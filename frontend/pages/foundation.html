Questions

      
Before you start.

      

      
What is Huma?
Huma is a free writing app that records how you type, then gives you a link and a PDF Report showing a person wrote the text, not AI.

      
How is Huma different from an AI detector?
AI detectors read finished text and guess. Huma doesn’t guess from the words: it records the writing as it happens, so the proof comes from how the text was made.

      
Can my teacher or client check it?
Yes. Send them the link. It opens in any browser with no account, and anyone can check it again at huma.social/verify.

      
Is Huma free?
Yes, on the web and on Mac.

      
Do I need an account?
Not to write. You sign in when you press Finish, so your page gets its link.

      
Is my writing private?
Your proof is made on your own computer. Nobody sees a page until you share it, and you can take a shared page down at any time.

      
What if I paste text?
You can. Pasted text is marked as pasted, so readers see how much you typed yourself.

      
Can someone retype AI text by hand?
Huma proves a person typed the text; it can’t see where the ideas came from. Readers can watch the writing replay, where drafting, pauses and edits look very different from copying.

      
Which browsers work?
Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on a computer. The Mac app adds a stronger check: every key press is confirmed from your keyboard.

      
How long can a document be?
Up to 10,000 characters, about 1,500 words.
