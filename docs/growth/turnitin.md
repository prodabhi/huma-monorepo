# Positioning Against Turnitin

**Turnitin is already moving to process tracking.**
- **Turnitin Clarity:** announced at SXSW EDU on March 4, 2025, and generally available from July 15, 2025.
  - It's a paid add-on for schools that already have Turnitin Feedback Studio.
  - Students draft in Turnitin's writing space. Teachers see pasted text, typing patterns, time spent and draft history, plus an AI assistant.
  - UCLA is trialling it for the Winter and Spring 2026 terms.
  - Critics raise student privacy concerns.
  - Sources:
    - https://www.turnitin.co.uk/press/turnitin-launches-turnitin-clarity-bringing-transparency-and-integrity-insights-to-education
    - https://dts.ucla.edu/initiatives/bruin-learn-center-excellence/academic-tech-tools/turnitin-clarity
    - https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/03/06/turnitin-announces-clarity-aim-to-make-student-writing-more-transparent/amp/

**What this means for Huma:** the market leader agrees that process beats detection. Huma's angle is:
- **Owned by the writer, not the school.** Free, with no school licence. Works for any teacher, client or editor.
- **The student decides who sees it.** They send a link; nobody watches them write.
- **Checkable by anyone, without trusting a company** (huma.social/verify).
- **Not limited to schools.** Freelancers, journalists, job applications and newsletters can all use it.
