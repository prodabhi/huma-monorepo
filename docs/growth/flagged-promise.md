# "Flagged? We've Got Your Back"

**The idea:** if you write in Huma and someone accuses you of using AI, Huma helps you prove it. No AI detector does this, because a detector can't back up its own guess. Huma can, because it has a record.

### What we actually promise (keep it to things we can always do)

1. **We explain your proof to them.** Send us the teacher's, editor's or client's email and your Huma link. We write to them directly and explain what the link shows and how to check it at huma.social/verify.
2. **We give you an appeal pack:**
   - Your Report (PDF).
   - A one-page "How to read a Huma Report" for the reader.
   - The research on detector errors (Post A).
   - A short appeal letter you can edit.
3. **We answer within 2 business days**, at team@huma.social with the subject "Flagged".

### What we don't promise (say it plainly; it protects you and builds trust)
- We can't change a school's decision, and we aren't lawyers. This isn't legal advice.
- We stand behind what the record shows, nothing more. Pasted parts are shown as pasted, and we don't vouch for them.
- We only see a document if you send us its link.

### Who it covers
Anything written in Huma with a link created at Finish. Only the writer can ask us.

### Blog angles from this
- **"What Happens When a Huma Writer Gets Flagged"**: walk through a real case once you have one, with permission and no names.
- **"How to Appeal an AI Detection Result"**: the general guide for everyone (search: "how to appeal ai detection"), ending with "write in Huma and we'll do this with you".
- **"Why No AI Detector Will Defend Its Own Result"**: a short opinion piece, which also works as a Reddit or HN post.

### Before launching
- **Capacity:** decide who answers these emails and how many a week you can handle.
- **Records:** keep a simple log of cases (date, school or client type, outcome). Real outcomes become your best marketing ("X writers cleared").
- **Legal:** have the lawyer reviewing the Terms also check this wording.
