# Process, Not Prediction: How Huma Proves You Wrote It

**Checked** = read in a primary or reliable source on 2026-11-05. **Unverified** = open the source yourself before publishing.

Use the CTA boxes from writing-rules.md in this post.

**Angle:** your flagship explainer. Keep it plain. Mention cryptography only as "anyone can check it without trusting us".

### What to explain (confirm wording with me)
- Huma records each change as you type, with its time, on your device.
- Paste is allowed but marked, so the reader sees how much you typed yourself.
- Finish creates a proof on your own device (not on Huma's servers), then a link and a PDF Report.
- The reader sees the % typed, the time spent and, optionally, a replay.
- Anyone can check the link at huma.social/verify. Changing the text after Finish breaks the check.
- What the reader doesn't see: anything you didn't put in the document.
