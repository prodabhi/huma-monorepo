# An Alternative to AI Detectors for Teachers

**Checked** = read in a primary or reliable source on 2026-11-05. **Unverified** = open the source yourself before publishing.

Use the CTA boxes from writing-rules.md in this post.

### Points
- **The cost of being wrong:** a wrongly accused student loses trust, and the class loses time on appeals.
- **Ask for process, not polish:** "Write it in Huma and send the link." The student keeps control and the teacher gets evidence.
- **Policy template idea:** "Submissions may include a Huma link. A detector score alone is not evidence."
- **Universities that switched detectors off:** Vanderbilt (checked); others unverified.
- **Honest limits:** Huma shows how text was typed. It doesn't judge quality, and a student could retype something. Pair it with conversation.
