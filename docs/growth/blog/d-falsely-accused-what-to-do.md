### Steps (practical, no legal advice)
1. **Stay calm and ask for specifics:** which tool, what score, and what the school's policy says about detector results.
2. **Gather evidence:**
   - Version history in Google Docs (File → Version history) or Word (OneDrive version history).
   - Drafts, notes, your browser history of sources, and timestamps.
3. **Offer to explain the work in person.** Authors can explain their choices.
4. **Cite the research:** OpenAI's shutdown, the Stanford study, Vanderbilt. Your Post A is the citation pack.
5. **Ask for a human review.** Many policies say a detector score alone isn't proof. Check yours.
6. **Next time, write in Huma** and attach the link.
