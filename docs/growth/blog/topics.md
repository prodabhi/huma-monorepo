# Blog Topic Ideas

Topics beyond the seven briefs (A–G), grouped by what the reader is doing when they search. Each one makes the same point from a different angle: **detectors guess, Huma shows how it was written, and anyone can check it.**

How to read each row:
- **Search:** the main phrase to target, which goes in the title, the URL and the first paragraph.
- **Why it sells Huma:** the moment in the post where Huma is the obvious answer.
- Facts come from `sources.md` or the matching brief. Anything new gets checked before it's published.

Every post: write it in Huma, end with the proof link, and use both CTA boxes from `writing-rules.md`.

Then one post a week from sections 2, 3 and 6, and the data study when you have the volunteers.

## Avoid

- "Beat Turnitin", "bypass AI detection", "undetectable": it brings in cheaters and loses teachers.
- Claiming any detector's accuracy without a source and date.
- Calling a competitor dishonest. Say what it does, what it doesn't, and let the reader decide.
