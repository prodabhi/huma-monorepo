# Huma vs Grammarly Authorship

**Checked** = read in a primary or reliable source on 2026-11-05. **Unverified** = open the source yourself before publishing.

Use the CTA boxes from writing-rules.md in this post.

**Angle:**
- Both record the writing process instead of guessing from the finished text. Credit Grammarly for that, since it validates your approach.
- The differences are who you have to trust, where you write, and what the company also sells.

### Grammarly Authorship facts (checked unless marked)

**Launch and where it works**
- Announced August 14, 2024, as a beta in Google Docs.
- Word and Pages were promised by the end of 2024.
- Source: https://www.grammarly.com/blog/company/grammarly-launches-grammarly-authorship/

**How it works**
- In Google Docs it works through the Grammarly browser extension, and in Word through the Grammarly desktop app.
- It labels text by where it came from: typed by a human, AI-generated (from Grammarly), pasted from a known or unknown source, and edited with Grammarly suggestions.
- Pasted-text sources depend on clipboard permission. Without it, pasted text shows as "unknown source".
- A replay shows how the document was written.
- A colour-coded report can be shared with a link. Grammarly doesn't send it to anyone automatically.
- Sources: https://www.grammarly.com/authorship and https://www.grammarly.com/blog/product/grammarly-authorship/

**Integrations**
- Canvas: https://www.instructure.com/resources/blog/grammarly-authorship-now-integrated-canvas-lms
- Blackboard: https://www.grammarly.com/blog/product/grammarly-authorship-is-now-available-in-blackboard/
- Named a Fast Company "Next Big Things in Tech" 2025.

**Known weaknesses**
- **Humanizer test:** Plagiarism Today (November 2025) found text rewritten by Grammarly's "Humanize" feature was marked "Typed by a Human".
- **Browser only:** in Google Docs it relies on the browser extension, so it can't see anything outside the browser.
- Source: https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/11/06/how-grammarly-launders-ai-generated-content/

**The company**
- In October 2025 Grammarly Inc. renamed itself **Superhuman**, after buying the Superhuman email app.
- It launched "Superhuman Go", an AI assistant, plus AI writing agents.
- **The point:** the company verifying your human writing also sells AI that writes for you.
- Sources: https://betakit.com/grammarly-rebrands-to-superhuman-as-it-expands-from-catching-typos-to-ai-productivity/ and https://www.fastcompany.com/91427120/inside-the-superhuman-effort-to-rebrand-grammarly

**Unverified (check before publishing)**

- Whether the reader needs a Grammarly account to open a report.
- Whether a report can be checked independently, or only trusted because Grammarly hosts it.
- Whether it works on a Mac outside the browser.
- Whether it still requires Google Docs or Word.

### Huma facts (from our code; confirm the wording with me before publishing)
- **Where you write:** Huma is its own editor, on the web or the free Mac app. No extension, and no Google account needed.
- **What it records:** every change, with its time. Pasted text is marked as pasted.
- **Automated typing:** if text appears without key presses (scripts, auto-typers), Huma stops the recording with "Automated typing detected".
- **What you share:** press Finish to get a link and a PDF Report. The reader opens it in any browser with no account. It shows how much was typed versus pasted and a replay of the writing (if you choose to show it).
- **Checking:** anyone can check a link at huma.social/verify. The proof is made on the writer's own device, not on Huma's server, and can be checked without trusting Huma.
- **What Huma doesn't do:** Huma doesn't sell AI writing.
- **Pricing:** free, on the web and on Mac.

### Honest gaps (say them; it builds trust)
- With Huma you write in a new editor, not inside Google Docs or Word.
- Huma has no Canvas or Blackboard integration (yet).
- Neither tool can tell whether a person typed out text they read somewhere else. Huma shows the timing, so unnatural, steady retyping is visible to a reader. Don't claim Huma catches it automatically unless we build that.
