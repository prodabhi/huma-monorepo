# Growth

Everything about getting Huma found and chosen.

**The goal:** be the way people prove they wrote something, instead of AI detectors (Turnitin, GPTZero) and Grammarly Authorship.

**The three messages:**
1. AI detectors guess, and they get it wrong (sources in `sources.md`).
2. Huma doesn't guess: it shows how the text was written, and anyone can check it.
3. Unlike Grammarly Authorship, Huma is checkable without trusting us, and Huma doesn't sell AI writing.

## How the blog works

1. Write each post **in Huma** and end it with "Written in Huma" and the proof link.
2. Use both CTA boxes from `blog/writing-rules.md`: "It's free. Start Writing."
3. Every number links to its source. Anything marked **Unverified** gets checked before it's published.
