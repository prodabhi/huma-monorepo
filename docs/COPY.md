## App onboarding

### Screen 1 · Welcome
Title: Prove you wrote it.
Body: Write in Huma. Get a link that shows you wrote it.
Buttons: Not now · Next

### Screen 2 · Touch ID
Title: Touch ID shows it's you.
Body: To show you're at your Mac as you write, Huma asks for your fingerprint when necessary. It never leaves this Mac.
Status when ready: Touch ID is ready.
Status when no fingerprint: Add a fingerprint in System Settings.
Status when off: Touch ID is off. Turn it on in System Settings.
Status when the Mac can't: This Mac can't make proofs. It has no Touch ID chip.
Buttons: Back · Next

### Screen 3 · Sign in (required)
Title: Sign in.
Body: To get your links, sign in with Google, Apple or email.
Button before: Sign in
Status while waiting: Finish signing in in your browser.
Status when done: Signed in as {email}.
Status when it fails: Sign-in didn't finish. Try again.
Small print: You don't need crypto or money.
Buttons: Back · Next (only works once signed in)

### Screen 4 · Finish and share
Title: Press Finish to share.
Body: When you're done, press Finish. Huma gives you a link and records it on Base.
Button: Start writing
